About

PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering with expertise in computational fluid dynamics (CFD), magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), heat transfer, and multiphysics flow modeling. My doctoral research focuses on high-fidelity numerical simulation of complex flow geometries including sudden expansions, bends, T-junctions, and disaligned channels, with particular emphasis on liquid metal MHD flows and heat transfer for fusion energy applications. Proficient in ANSYS Fluent, OpenFOAM, and finite volume method (FVM)-based solvers. Published 11+ peer-reviewed papers as first and corresponding author in SCIE/Scopus-indexed journals and actively serving as a reviewer for International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, Physics of Fluids, and Transport Phenomena.

Research Interests

Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) Heat Transfer Laminar & Turbulent Flow Liquid Metal Cooling Multiphysics Flow Modeling Thermal & Energy Systems Finite Volume Method (FVM) Flow Optimization (RSM)

Research Highlights

  • Developed dimensionless MHD models for sudden and gradual channel expansions, quantifying Hartmann and Reynolds number effects on pressure drop and velocity distributions
  • Investigated wall conductivity (insulating vs. conducting) and inclined magnetic field effects on MHD flow and heat transfer in square ducts relevant to fusion blanket channel design
  • Applied Response Surface Methodology (RSM) for optimization of thermal and hydrodynamic performance in MHD heat transfer configurations
  • Characterized electric current redistribution and Lorentz force localization in expansion geometries relevant to flow control in fusion cooling systems

Current Research

Currently investigating entropy generation in magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) channel flows to analyze thermodynamic irreversibilities arising from viscous effects. The work incorporates entropy-based analysis using parameters such as the Bejan number to distinguish between heat transfer and fluid friction contributions. The study aims to optimize flow and thermal performance in fusion-relevant channel configurations through a dimensionless framework.

About

I am a Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT), Bhopal, Under the supervision of Dr. Deepak Kumar. My research focuses on understanding the mechanical behavior, stability, failure, and mechanobiology of soft biological tissues through analytical modeling, constitutive theory, finite element simulations, and experimental validation. The primary objective of my Ph.D. research is to investigate the role of fiber–matrix and fiber-dispersion interactions in governing the deformation, instability, and failure mechanisms of biological tissues.

My proposed Ph.D. thesis is entitled “Role of Fiber-Matrix Coupling in the Mechanobiology of Tissues: Modeling with Experimental Validations.” The work combines theoretical mechanics, nonlinear elasticity, and experiments to establish structure–function relationships in soft tissues.

Research Highlights

Biomechanics
Mechanobiological investigation of soft biological tissues through constitutive modeling, finite element simulations, and experimental validation, with emphasis on fiber–matrix coupling, fiber dispersion, failure mechanisms, instability phenomena, and mullins stress-softening behavior in biological tissues.
Morpho-Elasticity
Analytical modeling of growth, remodeling, and structural failure mechanisms in complex biological systems like bamboo.
Renewable Energy
Exploring biomass gasification and the thermo-kinetic implications of pyrolysis-based sustainable energy production.
